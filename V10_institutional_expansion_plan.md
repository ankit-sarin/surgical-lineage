**Why highest priority:** Anchors 3-4 floating persons (Lillehei, Lewis, Barnard)

**Chair succession:** Wangensteen (~1930-1967) → Lillehei? → Najarian → current

### 1B. Cornell/New York Hospital (now Weill Cornell)
**Why high priority:** Anchors Lillehei (floating, was Stimson Professor 1967),

**Chair succession:** George Heuer (1932-1947) → Frank Glenn (1950s) →
C. Walton Lillehei (1967) → Tom Shires → Fabrizio Michelassi (current)
**Known persons in graph:** George Heuer (Halsted trainee, founded Cornell program),
C. Walton Lillehei (floating)
**Key claim:** Heuer established the nation's second modern surgical residency (1932).

surgery revolution from Minnesota to New York.

governance_leadership to existing Cincinnati department (currently only Mont Reid).
**Research needed:** Michelassi & Fahey, Am Surg 2009 (department history paper —
found via ResearchGate, need PMID).
